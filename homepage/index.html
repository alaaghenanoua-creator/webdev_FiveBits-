What is Ishara?

Ishara — Arabic for “sign” — is a platform that connects deaf and hard-of-hearing people with accessible services, sign language interpreters, and learning opportunities, built for Algeria.

Who can use the platform?

Individuals looking for accessible services or learning opportunities, interpreters, and organizations or professionals who want to make their services more accessible.

How can I find an interpreter?

Visit the interpreter directory to explore available profiles — languages, locations and specializations — as this information becomes available.

How can I register as a provider?

Choose Providers in the navigation to see who providers are and what they offer, then choose “Join Us” to sign up.

Are the courses suitable for beginners?

Yes — the learning area is designed to include beginner-friendly content and resources. Course details will be shown as they are published.
